## The 8kg Dumbbell Hack: "The 3-Second Rule"

Instead of dropping your reps, you will keep your reps around 10 to 12, but you will change how you lift:

- Take 3 seconds to lower the weight (e.g., lowering the dumbbells to your chest during a press).
- Pause for 1 second at the bottom.
- Explode up in 1 second.
